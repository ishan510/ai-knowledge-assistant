# Locked Northstar Accounts

Document ID: KB-003
Last updated: 2026-09-30
Owner: Support Operations

## Who this applies to
This policy applies to customers who sign in with an email
address and password. Customers using company single sign-on
(SSO) must contact their IT administrator about sign-in lockouts.

## Automatic account lockout
After five consecutive failed password attempts, Northstar
locks the account for 15 minutes.

Sign-in attempts during the lockout do not extend its duration.
After 15 minutes, the customer can try signing in again.

## Forgotten passwords
Customers who have forgotten their password should select
"Forgot password?" on the sign-in page.

Resetting a password does not end an active lockout.
The customer must still wait for the 15-minute lockout to end.

See KB-001: Resetting Your Northstar Password for reset
instructions and reset link expiration.

## Support actions
Support agents cannot manually remove an automatic lockout.
They can explain the waiting period and password reset process.

If the customer still cannot sign in after the lockout expires
and a password reset is completed, escalate to Technical Support.

## Suspected unauthorized access
If a customer reports failed sign-in attempts they did not make,
escalate immediately to the Security team.

Never ask the customer to share their password or reset link.
