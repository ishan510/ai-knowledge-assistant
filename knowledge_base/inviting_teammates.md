# Inviting Teammates to Northstar

Document ID: KB-008
Last updated: 2026-09-30
Owner: Customer Onboarding

## Who can invite teammates
Workspace owners and admins can invite teammates.
Members cannot send invitations.

Admins can invite users as admins or members.
Only owners can assign the owner role.

## Sending an invitation
1. Open Settings → Members.
2. Select "Invite teammate."
3. Enter the teammate's email address.
4. Choose their workspace role.
5. Select "Send invitation."

The teammate must sign in or create an account using
the same email address that received the invitation.

## Invitation expiration
Invitations expire after 7 days.
Owners and admins can resend pending invitations from
Settings → Members.

Resending an invitation invalidates the previous link
and starts a new 7-day expiration period.

## User limits
Active users and pending invitations both count toward
the workspace's user limit.

If the limit is reached, remove an unnecessary pending
invitation or ask the workspace owner to upgrade the plan.

See KB-005: Northstar Subscription Plans for user limits.

## If the invitation email does not arrive
- Wait up to five minutes.
- Check the spam or junk folder.
- Ask the inviter to verify the email address.
- Ask the inviter to resend the invitation if needed.

If the email still does not arrive, contact Northstar Support.

## Canceling an invitation
Owners and admins can cancel pending invitations from
Settings → Members. Canceled invitation links stop working
immediately.
