# Resetting Your Northstar Password

Document ID: KB-001
Last updated: 2026-09-30
Owner: Support Operations

## Who this applies to
Customers who sign in using an email address and password.
Customers using company single sign-on (SSO) must contact
their company's IT administrator to reset their password.

## Reset instructions
1. Open the Northstar sign-in page.
2. Select "Forgot password?"
3. Enter the email address associated with your account.
4. Open the password reset email and select the reset link.
5. Choose a new password with at least 12 characters.
6. Return to the sign-in page and sign in.

## Reset link expiration
Password reset links expire after 30 minutes and can be used
only once. Requesting a new link invalidates previous links.

## If the email does not arrive
- Wait up to five minutes.
- Check your spam or junk folder.
- Confirm you entered the email address used for your account.
- If it still has not arrived, contact Northstar Support.

## Security rules
Support agents must never ask customers for their passwords
or password reset links.
