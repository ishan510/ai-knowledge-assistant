# Northstar Refund Policy

Document ID: KB-002
Last updated: 2026-09-30
Owner: Billing Operations

## Who this applies to
This policy covers customers on Northstar's standard monthly
and annual subscriptions. Enterprise contracts follow the
refund terms in their signed agreement.

## Monthly subscriptions
Monthly subscription payments are non-refundable.
Customers who cancel retain access until the end of their
current paid billing period.

## Annual subscriptions
Customers may request a full refund within 14 calendar days
of their initial annual subscription purchase.

After 14 calendar days, annual subscriptions are non-refundable.
This refund window does not apply to annual renewals.

## Duplicate charges
An accidental duplicate charge is eligible for a refund
after the billing team verifies it.

## How to request a refund
Contact Northstar Support using the email address associated
with the account. Include the invoice number and the reason
for the request. Never include full payment card details.

Support agents must send refund requests to Billing Operations.
Support agents cannot approve or issue refunds themselves.

## Processing time
Once approved, refunds typically appear on the original
payment method within 5–10 business days.
