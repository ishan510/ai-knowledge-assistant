# Northstar Subscription Plans

Document ID: KB-005
Last updated: 2026-09-30
Owner: Billing Operations

## Standard plans
Northstar offers two standard subscription plans:

- Starter: up to 5 users and 10 GB of workspace storage.
- Team: up to 25 users and 100 GB of workspace storage.

Both plans are available with monthly or annual billing.
This document does not specify subscription prices.

## Enterprise plans
Enterprise user limits, storage limits, pricing, and billing
terms are defined in the customer's signed agreement.

Support agents must refer contract-specific questions to
the customer's account manager.

## Managing a subscription
Only workspace owners can change the subscription.
Open Settings → Billing → Manage plan.

## Upgrades
Upgrades take effect immediately.
Before confirmation, the billing page shows any prorated
charge for the remainder of the current billing period.

## Downgrades
Downgrades take effect at the next renewal.

Before scheduling a downgrade, the workspace must meet
the new plan's user and storage limits. Northstar does not
automatically delete users or files to meet those limits.

## Cancellation
Cancellation stops the next renewal.
The workspace retains access until the end of the current
paid billing period.

Cancellation does not automatically issue a refund.
See KB-002: Northstar Refund Policy for refund eligibility.

## Questions about charges
Support agents may explain the plan rules but must escalate
disputed charges to Billing Operations.
