# Failed Subscription Payments

Document ID: KB-006
Last updated: 2026-09-30
Owner: Billing Operations

## Who this applies to
This policy applies to standard monthly and annual subscriptions
paid by card. Enterprise invoice payments follow the customer's
signed agreement.

## Payment notifications
If a subscription payment fails, Northstar emails the workspace
owner with instructions to update the payment method.

## Automatic retries
Northstar retries the payment 3 days and 7 days after the
initial failure. A successful payment stops further retries.

## Access during the grace period
The workspace retains full access for 7 days after the initial
payment failure.

If the payment remains unpaid after the final retry on day 7,
the workspace becomes read-only. Users can view existing content
but cannot create or edit it.

## Updating the payment method
Only workspace owners can update billing information.

1. Open Settings → Billing → Payment method.
2. Update the card details.
3. Select "Pay outstanding invoice" to retry the payment.

Saving a new payment method does not itself trigger a payment.

## Restoring access
Full access is restored after the outstanding payment succeeds.

If the workspace remains read-only more than one hour after
successful payment, contact Northstar Support with the invoice
number.

## Support actions
Support agents must never request full card numbers or card
security codes.

If payment continues to fail, advise the customer to contact
their card issuer and escalate the case to Billing Operations.

This document does not specify how long unpaid workspace data
is retained.
